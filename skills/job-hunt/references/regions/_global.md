# Region pack: Global / fallback

Use when no specific pack exists, alongside any local knowledge the user gives during setup.

## Job boards
- LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed (country domain), Glassdoor jobs
- Company career pages (often the freshest and the only place some roles appear)
- Remote: We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Remotive, Wellfound (check each listing's allowed countries)
- Non-profit / development: Idealist, ReliefWeb, Devex
- Academic: university career pages, THE Unijobs, jobs.ac.uk (UK-focused, international listings)

## Employee-review sources
- Glassdoor, Indeed company reviews, Reddit, Blind (tech)

## Pay conventions
- Ask the user how salaries are quoted where they live. Record currency and period in rules.md.
- "Remote worldwide" listings often pay by country band. Read the fine print.

## Red flags anywhere
- Any fee to apply, train or receive equipment
- Interviews conducted only over chat apps, offers without an interview
- Requests for ID or bank details before a written offer
- Company with no verifiable address, site or employees
