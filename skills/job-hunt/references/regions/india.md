# Region pack: India

## Job boards
- General: Naukri, foundit (formerly Monster India), LinkedIn, Indeed India, Shine
- Startups / tech: Instahyre, Cutshort, Wellfound, Hirist, iimjobs (management roles)
- Government / public sector: National Career Service portal (ncs.gov.in), Employment News, state PSC sites
- Academic: university and college career pages, UGC and state notifications, Employment News
- Company career pages, especially for MNC GCCs and agencies

## Employee-review sources
- AmbitionBox (largest India-specific volume), Glassdoor, Indeed reviews
- Reddit: city and industry subreddits, searched by company name

## Pay conventions
- Offers are quoted as CTC in LPA (lakhs per annum). Many users think in monthly in-hand. Record which one rules.md uses and convert every listing to it.
- CTC often bundles employer PF, gratuity, insurance and variable pay. Monthly in-hand can be noticeably lower than CTC ÷ 12. When a listing gives only CTC, say the in-hand figure is an estimate.
- Many listings say "as per industry standards" or "best in industry". Treat as Undisclosed.

## Work norms worth checking
- Five-day weeks are common in corporate roles; six-day weeks remain common in agencies, SMEs and some sectors. Flag them.
- Notice periods of 30 to 90 days are normal. Profiles should state theirs, since forms ask.
- Rotational or "US shift" roles mean night work. Flag unless the user accepts it.

## Region-specific red flags
- Employment bonds or security deposits, often with penalty clauses
- Employer retaining original certificates or documents
- Registration, training or "kit" fees from placement agencies
- Delayed salary patterns on AmbitionBox
- Sales or cold-calling targets hidden inside "marketing executive" JDs

## Work authorisation notes
- Rarely relevant for Indian citizens working in India. Overseas roles: see the relevant country pack.
