# Estimated ATS Compatibility rubric

Use only when comparing a resume against a specific, sufficiently complete job description.

There is no universal ATS score. This rubric estimates alignment and parsing readiness; it does not reproduce Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, Taleo, or any employer's private ranking logic.

Score out of 100:

| Dimension | Weight | Guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| JD skill & keyword alignment | 30 | Reward genuinely supported concepts and terminology, not repetition |
| Experience relevance | 25 | How closely confirmed scope/responsibilities map to the role's core work |
| Evidence & impact | 15 | Strength and specificity of proof for relevant capabilities |
| Parsing & structure safety | 10 | Whether a conventional parser is likely to recover core sections and text |
| Role/title alignment | 10 | Transferability of past/current roles to the target; do not punish reasonable title differences excessively |
| Education / licenses / hard requirements | 5 | Score only requirements actually stated or clearly material to the JD |
| Clarity & concision | 5 | Relevant information is easy to find and not buried in noise |

## Interpretation

- 85–100: very strong documented alignment
- 75–84: strong alignment with a few gaps
- 65–74: plausible stretch; tailoring and/or real gaps remain
- 50–64: material mismatch or under-evidenced fit
- below 50: weak documented alignment

These are analysis bands, not interview probabilities.

## Hard-requirement handling

If the JD contains a true gate such as a mandatory professional license, security clearance, work authorization, language, degree, or location requirement, surface it separately. A high keyword score cannot override an unmet hard requirement.

## Keyword handling

Classify JD terms as:
- exact confirmed match
- semantic confirmed match
- confirmed but absent from resume
- unconfirmed
- not possessed

Only the first three can drive resume changes. Put the last two into gaps / DO NOT ADD.
