# Region pack: United Kingdom

## Job boards
- General: Indeed UK, LinkedIn, Reed, Totaljobs, CV-Library, Guardian Jobs
- Government / public sector: Civil Service Jobs, NHS Jobs, local council sites
- Academic / research: jobs.ac.uk, university career pages
- Non-profit: CharityJob
- Creative / media: Guardian Jobs, The Dots

## Employee-review sources
- Glassdoor, Indeed reviews, Reddit (r/UKJobs and industry subreddits)

## Pay conventions
- Annual gross salary in GBP, often as a band. London roles sometimes add a London weighting.
- Public-sector and university roles use published pay scales or grades. Look up the grade when only the grade is listed.

## Work norms worth checking
- Standard week is about 35 to 40 hours. Hybrid is common.
- Notice periods: one month is typical, longer for senior roles.
- Probation of three to six months is common.

## Region-specific red flags
- "Self-employed" or commission-only framing for what is really a staff role
- Unpaid trial shifts or extended unpaid tasks

## Work authorisation notes
- Candidates who need a visa should check whether the employer appears on the UK government's register of licensed sponsors (published on gov.uk), and whether the role meets current Skilled Worker salary and skill thresholds, which change. Treat sponsorship as unknown unless the listing states it.
