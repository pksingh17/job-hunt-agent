# Region pack: Germany

## Job boards
- General: StepStone, Indeed DE, LinkedIn, XING
- Government: the Federal Employment Agency's Jobbörse (Bundesagentur für Arbeit)
- Academic / research: academics.de, university career pages, EURAXESS (EU-wide research jobs)
- English-language roles: filter by language, or search in English on LinkedIn and StepStone

## Employee-review sources
- Kununu (dominant locally), Glassdoor

## Pay conventions
- Annual gross salary in EUR (Bruttojahresgehalt). Net take-home is much lower after tax and social contributions.
- Many listings omit salary. Treat as Undisclosed.

## Work norms worth checking
- German language level is often required even when the JD is in English. Check for "Deutschkenntnisse" or a CEFR level.
- Probation (Probezeit) is commonly six months.
- Notice periods can be one to three months.

## Region-specific red flags
- Fixed-term contracts (befristet) chained repeatedly
- Unpaid "Probearbeit" beyond a short trial

## Work authorisation notes
- Non-EU candidates should check the EU Blue Card and skilled-worker routes on official German government sites. Thresholds change, so verify current figures at the time of applying.
