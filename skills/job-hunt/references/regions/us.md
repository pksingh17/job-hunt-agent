# Region pack: United States

## Job boards
- General: LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor jobs
- Startups / tech: Wellfound, Built In (city editions), Y Combinator's Work at a Startup
- Government: USAJOBS, state and city job portals
- Academic: HigherEdJobs, Chronicle of Higher Education jobs, university HR sites
- Non-profit: Idealist

## Employee-review sources
- Glassdoor, Indeed reviews, Blind (tech), Reddit

## Pay conventions
- Annual gross USD for salaried roles, hourly for others.
- Several states and cities require pay ranges in listings. A missing range in those places is itself a small signal.
- Benefits (health insurance especially) matter a great deal to total compensation. Note them when listed.

## Work norms worth checking
- At-will employment is the norm. Notice of two weeks is customary.
- "Unlimited PTO" varies widely in practice. Reviews tell you more than the policy.

## Region-specific red flags
- 1099 contractor framing for full-time work
- Reshipping, payment-processing or "check cashing" roles (classic scams)

## Work authorisation notes
- Candidates needing sponsorship should look for explicit sponsorship language and treat silence as unknown.
