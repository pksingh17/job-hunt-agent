# Resume Health rubric

This is an internal quality rubric, not an employer or ATS score.

Score out of 100:

| Dimension | Weight | What good looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| Structure & parsing safety | 20 | Clear sections, selectable text, conventional chronology, core content not trapped in decorative elements |
| Relevance & positioning | 20 | Target direction is obvious and the most relevant evidence is prominent |
| Evidence & impact | 20 | Claims are specific, supported, and quantified only where the candidate can verify numbers |
| Bullet quality | 15 | Strong action + scope/context + result or purpose; little filler |
| Skills architecture | 10 | Skills are truthful, grouped sensibly, and consistent with experience |
| Clarity & concision | 10 | Fast to scan, low redundancy, precise language |
| Consistency & hygiene | 5 | Dates, tense, punctuation, titles, links, spelling and formatting are consistent |

Interpretation:
- 90–100: strong baseline; optimize only for specific roles
- 80–89: good, with a few material improvements
- 70–79: workable but several changes could improve clarity/relevance
- 60–69: significant weaknesses
- below 60: rebuild or substantial revision recommended

Never present these bands as probabilities of selection.
